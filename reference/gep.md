# Global Education Profiler *(random sample)*

A dataset containing information about students' *social integration
(si)* experience at their Higher Education Institutions and their
*communication skills (cs)* development. For *cs*, scores of 'actualy
experience' and 'importance to me' are available. This dataset only
contains a random sample of 300 observations out of well over 12,000
students as of August 2021.

## Usage

``` r
gep
```

## Format

A data frame with 300 rows and 12 variables:

- age:

  *Age of participant*

- gender:

  *Gender of participant*

- level_of_study:

  *Degree level at the time of the research project*

- si_socialise_with_people_exp:

  *Extent to which participants have opportunities to socialise with
  people from diverse cultural backgrounds* \| 1 ("not at all") to 6
  ("very much")

- si_supportive_friends_exp:

  *Extent to which participants have supportive friends with a diverse
  cultural background* \| 1 ("not at all") to 6 ("very much")

- si_time_socialising_exp:

  *Extent to which participants take time to socialise with people from
  diverse cultural backgrounds* \| 1 ("not at all") to 6 ("very much")

- cs_explain_ideas_imp:

  *Extent to which participants perceive improving in explaining ideas
  to others (importance)* \| 1 ("not at all") to 6 ("very much")

- cs_find_clarification_imp:

  *Extent to which participants are able to clarify what others mean
  (importance)* \| 1 ("not at all") to 6 ("very much")

- cs_learn_different_styles_imp:

  *Extent to which participants learn about other students communication
  styles (importance)* \| 1 ("not at all") to 6 ("very much")

- cs_explain_ideas_exp:

  *Extent to which participants perceive improving in explaining ideas
  to others (experience)* \| 1 ("not at all") to 6 ("very much")

- cs_find_clarification_exp:

  *Extent to which participants are able to clarify what others mean
  (experience)* \| 1 ("not at all") to 6 ("very much")

- cs_learn_different_styles_exp:

  *Extent to which participants learn about other students communication
  styles (experience)* \| 1 ("not at all") to 6 ("very much")
