# IC Training Data

A dataset which contains real-world pre- and post-training test scores
for Intercultural Communication trainees. Participants names were
replaced with random names.

## Usage

``` r
ic_training
```

## Format

A data frame with 96 rows and 11 variables:

- name:

  Random names created with `randomName` package

- test:

  Defines whether a test was performed before or after training

- communication:

  Level of confidence in communication with people from diverse cultural
  backgrounds

- teamwork:

  Level of confidence in working with people from diverse cultural
  backgrounds

- leadership:

  Level of confidence in leading people from diverse cultural
  backgrounds

- communication2:

  Factorised 'communication' with levels "yes and "no"

- teamwork2:

  Factorised 'teamwork' with levels "yes and "no"

- leadership2:

  Factorised 'leadership' with levels "yes and "no"

- communication3:

  Factorised 'communication' with levels "high", "medium", and "low"

- teamwork3:

  Factorised 'teamwork' with levels "high", "medium", and "low"

- leadership3:

  Factorised 'leadership' with levels "high", "medium", and "low"

## Source

Research conducted at the University of Warwick (2018)
